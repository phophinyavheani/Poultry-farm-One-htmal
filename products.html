Our Products

Nyavhas M Poultry Farm supplies fresh, healthy and affordable poultry products to customers in Thononda Village and the surrounding communities. We are committed to providing high-quality products while maintaining excellent customer service.

Fresh Eggs

Our farm produces fresh eggs daily. The eggs are carefully collected, cleaned and packed to ensure quality and freshness for every customer.

  • Fresh daily
  • Available in trays
  • Affordable prices

Live Chickens

Healthy live chickens are available for customers who wish to purchase birds for personal use, farming or traditional ceremonies.

Price R120 per chicken

  • Different sizes available
  • Suitable for households and small businesses

Cleaned Ready-to-Cook Chicken

We also provide professionally cleaned chickens that are ready for cooking, making meal preparation quick and convenient for our customers.

  • Ready for cooking
