About Us

Nyavhas M Poultry Farm was established in 2022 in Thononda Village, Limpopo. The farm began as a small home-based poultry business with the aim of supplying fresh and affordable poultry products to the local community.The business starts as a home based farming project with a limited number of chickens and continues to increase to consist of two sections focused to poultry. While the other portion raises broiler chickens for meat production, the first area concentrates on producing eggs.

Over time, the business has grown to serve neighbouring villages by providing fresh eggs, live chickens and cleaned ready-to-cook chicken. We are committed to quality, excellent customer service and supporting local households with reliable poultry products.

With consistent growth, the business now largely depends on direct customer visits and word of mouth promotion. It does not yet have an official website where customers can learn about available products, enquire about prices or contact the owner online. Developing a professional website will improve the farms online presence, increase customer engagement and make it easier for new and existing customers to access information.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide fresh, affordable and high quality poultry products while delivering excellent customer service and supporting food security within our local communities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the leading trusted poultry farms in Limpopo by supplying quality products, expanding our services and embracing modern technology to better serve our customers.
