Welcome to Nyavhas M Poultry Farm

Nyavhas M Poultry Farm is a local poultry business located in Thononda Village, Limpopo. We supply fresh eggs, live chickens, and cleaned ready-to-cook chicken to households, local vendors and surrounding communities.

Our Products

  • Fresh Eggs
  • Live Chickens
  • Cleaned Chicken

We are committed to providing affordable, high-quality poultry products while maintaining excellent customer service.
